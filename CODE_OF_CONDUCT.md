# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We expect participants to act with empathy, kindness, and respect, and to give
and accept constructive feedback responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or
imagery, trolling, and publishing another person's private information without
permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement and reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `siebrand@kitano.nl`. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and confidentially. Project maintainers may remove or reject
contributions that violate this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
